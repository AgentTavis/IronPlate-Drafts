The Welcome

Built in 2024. Run by Kristen. Like coming home since day one.

Kackalax is a brand-new wooden tavern with a porch full of log benches and a back yard built around an outdoor stone fireplace. Kristen opened the doors in 2024, hung the string lights, and stocked the shelf with bourbon she actually likes. The crowd showed up. The reviews followed. The rest is what you walk into when you pull up to 5509 Appomattox Rd.

Five-star Google reviews from 56 neighbors. A family-cooked Sunday spread out of Gammy's kitchen. Local bands on the patio. A community bar that means it.
