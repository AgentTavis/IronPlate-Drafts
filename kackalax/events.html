Live Music

Local bands. String lights. Cold beer.

Live music keeps the calendar going — local performers like Ray Martin Music headline most weekends. In good weather we set up out on the patio. In the cooler months the band rolls inside.

Friday & Saturday Nights

Most weekends we've got a band on the patio. Doors at 4 PM, music kicks off around 8. No cover most weeks. Bring a chair if you want one, we've got picnic tables and log benches but the crowd grows fast.
