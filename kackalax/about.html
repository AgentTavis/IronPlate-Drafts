Our Story

Like coming home.

Kristen opened Kackalax in 2024, with Gammy in the kitchen and a porch full of log benches. The neighborhood has been showing up ever since.

01 The Story

Pleasant Garden didn't have a place quite like this. Kristen saw a stretch of road on Appomattox, pictured a porch full of neighbors with cold drinks in their hands, and decided to build it. The sign out front says EST. 2024, and that's the truth, the bar is brand-new. The 5.0-star rating from 56 Google reviewers got built right alongside it.

The name? That's a story for the bar. Pull up a stool and ask Kristen, she'll tell you herself.

02 Gammy's Kitchen

Gammy cooks because Gammy cooks. Every Sunday, the smoker fires up out back, and the spread comes together while the NASCAR pre-race chatter rolls in off the TVs. Sunday Funday food is free, because that's how a Sunday is supposed to feel. The signature dish is Gammy's Cookout Casserole, but whatever's on the table that day is whatever Gammy felt like making.

It's not a menu. It's a family recipe and a Sunday afternoon.

03 The Building

Wooden timber frame. Metal roof. Dark-stained pine siding. Log benches on the porch and the address numbers painted white on a post out front. Walk to the back yard and you'll find a covered patio strung with warm bulbs, picnic tables under the trees, and a stone fireplace big enough to gather around in January.

Brick walls inside. Wood-paneled ceilings. TVs above the bar for the race. A dartboard. A real tap room with a real bourbon shelf. The building was built to feel like a front porch you don't want to leave.

04 The Community

Five-star rating. Fifty-six Google reviews. Three thousand nine hundred Facebook neighbors. Multi-generational tables, ugly Christmas sweaters in December, an Easter Bunny photo op for the kids in the spring. This isn't a destination, it's the place down the road where they know your drink and your sister's name.

Live music keeps the calendar moving, comedy keeps it laughing, bingo keeps it weird, and Gammy keeps it fed.

05 The Team

Kristen runs the floor. Gammy runs the kitchen. The staff knows your face by the second visit. Local performers like Ray Martin Music play the patio. The Pub & Grub Comedy Tour brings the laughs.

Want to play Kackalax with your band, host a private party, or just say hi? Drop us a line.
