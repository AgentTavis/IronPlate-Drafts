The signature plate

From the raw bar to the Low Country Boil.

A handful of dishes have followed us through every location. Order one and you'll understand why we keep them on every menu, every season.

No. 01

Chargrilled Oysters

Our flagship. Live-fired, butter-bathed, finished with a Southern bite that has carried this brand from the Triad to Atlantic Beach.

No. 02

Crawfish & Alligator Cheesecake

Savory, layered, unapologetically Southern. The dish that tells our regulars apart from our first-timers.

No. 03

Crab Stuffed Oysters

Half shell, half blue-crab pile, fully unreasonable. Order them once and they're yours forever.
