Service Capabilities

What we do.

From preconstruction through punch-list. Hands-on contracting from start to finish, with four trades self-performed in-house.

01 / GENERAL CONTRACTING

General Contracting

The primary service line. Hands-on contracting from start to finish across NC, SC, and VA. Annual contract volume over $100 million.

02 / DESIGN-BUILD

Design / Build

End-to-end design and construction integration. One team, one accountability. Streamlines the schedule and the budget by closing the gap between design intent and field execution.

03 / SELF-PERFORMANCE

Self-Performance

Concrete work. Steel erection. Carpentry. Plumbing. Four trades kept in-house. Rare in modern general contracting; foundational to the firm.

04 / PRECONSTRUCTION

Preconstruction

Led by Andrew E. Matthews, P.E., LEED AP. Estimating, planning, constructability review, and value engineering before the first shovel.

05 / PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Project Management

Led by Brian L. Roberts, VP of Project Management. 20+ years across institutional, medical, office, retail, and commercial work. Strong subcontractor and supplier rapport.

06 / LEED & SUSTAINABILITY

LEED-Accredited Construction

LEED AP professionals on staff. Sustainable and certified work delivered without subbing out the responsibility.

07 / SAFETY

Safety First Philosophy

A discipline, not a slogan. Every project, every day. Standing policy of the firm since the founding.

08 / TENURE

15+ Year Average Tenure

70 full-time staff. Stable team. Consistent client experience. The team that pulled your last permit is the team that will pull your next one.

09 / SURETY

35+ Year CNA Surety

One of the longest surety relationships in the region. A backing that supports the scale of work the firm takes on.
