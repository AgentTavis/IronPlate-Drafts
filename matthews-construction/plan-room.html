Subcontractor & Supplier Access

Requesting Plan Room access

Matthews Construction maintains an active plan room for subcontractors and suppliers bidding on current projects. To request access, or to submit a bid on a project that's currently open, get in touch with our preconstruction team and we will route you.

Please include the company name, trades or supply categories you cover, license information (where applicable), and the project or sector you're interested in.

Email Preconstruction → Call 828.464.7325

What we ask of new subs

  • Active license & insurance. Current GL, WC, and any trade-specific licensing in NC, SC, or VA per the project.
  • References. Three recent commercial references on similar scopes of work.
  • Capacity. Realistic scope you can crew, supply, and stand behind.
  • Safety record. EMR and recent safety history. Matthews operates on a Safety First philosophy.
