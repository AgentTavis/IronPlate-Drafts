The Founding Story

One family.
Seventy years.

Bobby founded Matthews Construction on a family tradition of providing honesty, integrity, and a guarantee of quality workmanship on each project. The business started at his home in Conover in 1955, with his wife Frankie handling administration. The company was incorporated in 1965.

By 2000, growth in the firm's mixed-use and hospitality portfolio pulled a second office south. The Charlotte division opened that year and has been steady ever since, run today by Nathan A. Matthews. The Conover office remains the firm's corporate headquarters.

Today the company runs annual contract volume over $100 million with 70 full-time staff. The average employee has been with Matthews for more than fifteen years. More than 50% of annual volume comes from repeat clients, the metric the firm has long held up as the best indicator of a general contractor's capability.
