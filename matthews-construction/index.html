Three Generations

One family. One standard.
Three generations on the same crew.

Bobby founded Matthews Construction in 1955 on a family tradition of honesty, integrity, and a guarantee of quality workmanship. Seventy years later, his sons and grandsons are still on the job.

01

Bobby E. Matthews

FOUNDER · 1928 — 2021

Founded the company at home with his wife Frankie handling administration. Incorporated 1965. Built the firm on hands-on contracting and a Safety First philosophy.

02

Gary & Nathan

GENERATION TWO · 1980s — PRESENT

Gary, President, runs day-to-day operations across both offices. Nathan, Executive VP, manages the Charlotte division opened in 2000. Forty-plus years of construction between them.

03

Andrew, Brian & Connor

GENERATION THREE · 2008 — PRESENT

Andrew, P.E., leads preconstruction. Brian heads project management. Connor, project engineer, is the next generation already on site. Carrying the standard forward.
