Marquee builds.

A closer look at a handful of projects that show the firm's range across hospitality, education, industrial, and mixed-use.

Hospitality · Hilton

Biltmore Hilton

ASHEVILLE, NC · GENERAL CONTRACTOR

A full-scale Hilton hotel in one of North Carolina's most demanding markets. The build represents the kind of large-format hospitality work that has put Matthews on the brand's preferred-contractor lists for Hilton, Marriott, Hyatt, and IHG.

Hospitality · Hyatt

Hyatt Place Asheville

ASHEVILLE, NC · GENERAL CONTRACTOR

Full-bleed exterior glazing, rooftop terrace, urban site. The Hyatt Place mark is a national brand prototype; Matthews delivered the Asheville build start-to-finish.

Education

Catawba Valley Community College

HICKORY, NC · GENERAL CONTRACTOR

A flagship higher-education build in the firm's home market. Andrew Matthews, P.E., serves on the SALT Block Foundation and Catawba County Chamber of Commerce boards in this same community.

Industrial

Schletter Inc

SOLAR MOUNTING · GENERAL CONTRACTOR

Heavy industrial facility for a multinational solar-mounting manufacturer. Self-performance of concrete and steel kept the schedule predictable on a build where the structure was the product.
