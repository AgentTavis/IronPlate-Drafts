The Story

Owner-operated. High Point's stage.

The Podium is Marcy Yarbray's house. She built it, she runs it, and she's behind every band booking, every menu line, every late-night last call.

Why Podium

Four concepts. One room.

Most sports bars pick a lane. The Podium runs four. There's a chef-driven kitchen putting out sixty-five dishes across six categories. There's a sports bar with forty-plus HD TVs running every game that matters. There's a real arcade, with pool tables, racing simulators, Skee-Ball, and pinball machines that get played hard. And there's a live music stage with a full PA, a real drum kit, and a hand-painted mural for a backdrop.

That mural is the brand. Trophy at the center. A banner across the top that reads "The Podium." A curve below it that reads "From Kickoff to Encore, We Pour." The three pillars across the base: Food. Entertainment. Drinks. It's painted, not printed. It's the room and it's the logo at the same time.

Walk in on a Tuesday and the tacos are two bucks. Walk in on a Friday and the kitchen's running the Fish Fry program with a dozen add-ons to dial in your plate. Walk in on a Saturday and a band's working through a set six feet in front of the mural while someone else is winning at Skee-Ball one room over.

It's reclaimed wood walls and Edison bulbs. It's neon beer signs and a bourbon shelf. It's families with kids at six, couples on dates at eight, bikers rolling in at ten, and the band at eleven. The Podium is built to hold all of it, all night.
